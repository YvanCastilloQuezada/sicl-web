# InfrastructureReadinessGate v1.0

Assess need, function, network, demand/capacity, territory, site/alignment, topography, geology, geotechnics, hydrology, seismicity, climate, environment, flows/loads, construction, operation, maintenance, standards, regulation, specialists and unknowns. Results include READY_FOR_SYSTEM_PLANNING, READY_FOR_CONCEPTUAL_DESIGN, RESEARCH_REQUIRED, DATA_REQUIRED, SITE_INVESTIGATION_REQUIRED, SPECIALIST_REQUIRED, NOT_READY and UNKNOWN.

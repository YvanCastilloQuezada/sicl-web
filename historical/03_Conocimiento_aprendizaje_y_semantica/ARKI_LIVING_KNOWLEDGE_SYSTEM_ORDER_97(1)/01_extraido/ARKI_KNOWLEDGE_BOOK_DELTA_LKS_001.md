# ARKI Knowledge Book Delta — ARKI-LKS-001

## Principle added

ARKI external knowledge is living, versioned and evidence-traceable. Researcher discovery and Curator verification are separate competencies; neither replaces Human Authority.

## Safety boundary

`SOURCE EXISTS ≠ CLAIM VERIFIED`. External information enters staged research evidence first. Canonical ARKI knowledge requires governed transitions and, for canonical promotion, Human Authority.

## Initial corpus

The first three living codebooks are IFC/openBIM, Smart Cities and Urban Digital Twins. Their source register records official/primary sources, version facts, mapping cautions and unknowns. No equivalence to ARKI is inferred from names.

## Automation boundary

The 15-day cycle is represented by a deterministic interface, but no scheduler or daemon is activated. Current state: `NOT DEMONSTRATED / EXTERNAL ORCHESTRATION REQUIRED`.

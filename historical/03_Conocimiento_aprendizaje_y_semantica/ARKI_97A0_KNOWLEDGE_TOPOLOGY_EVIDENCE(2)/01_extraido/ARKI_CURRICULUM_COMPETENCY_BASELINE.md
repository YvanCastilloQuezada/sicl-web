# ARKI 97A-0 — Curriculum and Competency Baseline

**Source of truth for this baseline:** AKI-001 diagnostic audit.  
**AKI-001 is not repeated or reimplemented.**

## Existing curriculum

AKI-001 reconstructed the historical sequence:

1. Geometric generation and families.
2. Semantic construction model.
3. BIM interface and workflow.
4. 2D/3D representation.
5. Persistence and interoperability.
6. Quantities, costs and planning.
7. Coordination, structures and MEP.
8. Computational architecture.

This is a reconstruction of what appeared in the repository and project history. It is not a claim that ARKI has professional mastery of every subject.

## Competency state rule

The following distinctions are preserved from the existing architecture:

```text
UNKNOWN
NOT_DEMONSTRATED
IMPLEMENTED
VERIFIED
E2E_VERIFIED
PROFESSIONAL_VALIDATION_REQUIRED
EXTERNAL_ORACLE_REQUIRED
```

A test for one fixture does not generalize automatically to a discipline, jurisdiction, scale or professional authorization.

## Academy/Tutor/Examiner/Oracle baseline

The existing review proposes the following future entities: `SourceRecord`, `KnowledgeClaim`, `ContradictionRecord`, `Competency`, `LearningUnit`, `HiddenExam`, `OracleCase`, `EvidenceRecord`, `CompetencyState` and `ProfessionalGate`. They are **contractual recommendations**, not existing executable structures. They are therefore recorded as migration requirements, not recreated here.

## Baseline verdict

AKI-001 supplies a usable diagnostic curriculum and competency baseline. It does not supply a runtime learning system, a standards registry, a canonical knowledge package, a hidden-exam engine or a professional certification system.

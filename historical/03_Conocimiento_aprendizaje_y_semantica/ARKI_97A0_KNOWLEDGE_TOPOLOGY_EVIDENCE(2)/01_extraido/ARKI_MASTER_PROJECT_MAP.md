## How to read the map

BKH is an ARKI internal program, not an industry standard. IFC and ISO 16739 are external references. `SceneModel` is a representation, not the semantic owner. BKH-1 relates; BKH-2 evaluates constraints; BKH-3 determines regeneration; BKH-4 governs transactions only in the authorized foundation scope. This separation is architectural orientation, not a claim that every later engine exists.

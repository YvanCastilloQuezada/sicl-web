# ARKI Master Codebook v1.0 — Canonical Approval

**Approval date:** 23 September 2026  
**Authority:** Product Owner / Human Authority  
**Decision:** APPROVED / CANONICAL

The Product Owner formally approved **ARKI Master Codebook v1.0** as the master reference for ARKI nomenclature.

The canonical rules are:

1. Historical codes are not renumbered or deleted.
2. Historical meanings marked `UNKNOWN` remain `UNKNOWN` until sufficient evidence is found.
3. New official ARKI codes must be registered in the Codebook or explicitly reference it.
4. A new identifier must not reuse an existing code with a different meaning.
5. Superseded and historical items remain traceable.
6. The Codebook approval does not authorize product, kernel, BKH, architecture or test changes.

The Codebook front is closed. Future changes require a documented delta and the applicable Human Authority approval.

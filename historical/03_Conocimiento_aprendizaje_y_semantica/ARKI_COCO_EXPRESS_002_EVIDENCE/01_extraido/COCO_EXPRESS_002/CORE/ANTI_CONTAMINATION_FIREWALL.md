# Anti-Contamination Firewall

Training material may use the published syllabus and ARKI's own verified contracts. It must not use expected answers, final fixtures, final values, copied assertions, or hardcoded logic from any DeepSeek/Lipsic exam.

Training fixtures and exam fixtures remain physically separate. Product code must not encode expected exam answers. Every transfer case must change context, identifiers, ordering, magnitudes or topology sufficiently to detect memorization.

The package records `DeepSeek_exam_contamination = NO` only after inspecting its own generated exercises and confirming they use the synthetic railway maintenance center scenario and independent fixtures.

# ARKI Standards → Curriculum Propagation v1.1

```text
STANDARD CHANGE → CURATOR → VERIFIED DELTA → KNOWLEDGE IMPACT
→ CURRICULUM IMPACT → COMPETENCY IMPACT → CAPABILITY IMPACT
→ IMPLEMENTATION IMPACT → HUMAN AUTHORITY
```

A change may produce `NO_IMPACT`, `POSSIBLE_IMPACT`, `MATERIAL_IMPACT`, `UNKNOWN` or `HUMAN_DECISION_REQUIRED`. Editorial changes and corrigenda that do not alter requirements must not create curriculum impact. Material requirement changes create a CurriculumDelta and competency revalidation analysis; they do not silently alter curriculum or product code.

Jurisdiction, scale, discipline and authority level must be checked before propagation. ISO is not automatically Peruvian law and CITY knowledge is not automatically BUILDING knowledge.

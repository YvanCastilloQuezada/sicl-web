## Status rules

`REGISTERED / DOCUMENTED` is not operational capability. A foundation dependency does not elevate a capability's runtime or verification status. Unknown and insufficient evidence remain non-pass states. Any mutation that cannot use the authorized boundary is `BLOCK + DECISION_REQUIRED`.

## Maximum status

`WAVE1_VERIFIED` is permitted only for the bounded foundation scope and only after independent review of the evidence package. This execution reports evidence; it does not self-declare independent verification.

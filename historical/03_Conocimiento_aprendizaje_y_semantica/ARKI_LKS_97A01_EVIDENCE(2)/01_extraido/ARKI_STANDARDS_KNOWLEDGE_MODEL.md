# ARKI Standards Knowledge Model v1.1

Standards are an LKS family, not a parallel Standards Registry. The model composes existing LKS sources, claims, evidence, curation, versioning and change detection.

Fields: `standardId`, organization, standard number, canonical title, edition, publication year/date, status, amendments, corrigenda, supersession, official source, domains, disciplines, scales, jurisdiction, authority level, related standards, concepts, requirements, indicators, typed ARKI mappings, curriculum/competency/capability/implementation mappings, evidence, last checked, curator status and unknowns.

Status distinguishes `CURRENT`, `PUBLISHED`, `AMENDED`, `CORRIGENDUM`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `UNDER_DEVELOPMENT`, `DRAFT`, `SUPERSEDED`, `WITHDRAWN` and `UNKNOWN`.

`DRAFT ≠ CURRENT STANDARD`; `STANDARD EXISTS ≠ REQUIREMENT APPLIES`; `STANDARD ≠ LAW ≠ REGULATION ≠ GUIDANCE`.

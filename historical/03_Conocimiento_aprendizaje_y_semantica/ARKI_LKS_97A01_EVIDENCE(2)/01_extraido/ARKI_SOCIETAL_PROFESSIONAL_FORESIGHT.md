# ARKI Societal and Professional Foresight v1.1

KCF may observe housing, health, education, accessibility, aging, climate adaptation, resilience, energy, water, mobility, public space, infrastructure, disaster risk, environmental quality, urbanization, territorial development and digital transformation. These are observation domains, not automatic curriculum.

KCF balances societal, professional, project, standards, regulatory, scientific, technology and strategic relevance. Commercial demand is a signal only; socially important needs may precede market demand.

Foresight remains multiscale across country, region, metropolitan system, city, district, neighborhood, public realm, site, building, system/zone, space, activity, object, component and human. Scale transfer is never automatic.

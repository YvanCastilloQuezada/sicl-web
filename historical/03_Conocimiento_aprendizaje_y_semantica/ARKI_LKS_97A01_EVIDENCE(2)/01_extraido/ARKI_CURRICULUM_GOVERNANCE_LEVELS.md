# ARKI Curriculum Governance Levels v1.1

| Level | Meaning | Examples | Automation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — Routine knowledge maintenance | Non-material maintenance within an approved line | Metadata, official link, corrigendum, minor update | May operate under approved policy and evidence |
| B — Approved curriculum evolution | Evolution within an authorized curriculum line | New IFC version, deeper approved competency, known revalidation | May prepare proposal inside approved limits |
| C — Strategic curriculum change | New discipline, major priority, scope or principle | New professional domain, new authority, strategic technology | `HUMAN_DECISION_REQUIRED` |

KCF proposes and prioritizes. Curator verifies evidence. Curriculum organizes. Tutor teaches. Examiner tests. Human Authority governs strategic direction. No level bypasses evidence or creates automatic product changes.

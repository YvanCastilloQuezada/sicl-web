# ARKI Governed Internet Research Policy v1.1

`INTERNET AVAILABLE ON DEMAND · RESEARCH ACTIVATED BY POLICY`.

Internet access is a capability, not an authority. External content is untrusted and follows `INTERNET → RESEARCHER → CLAIM → CURATOR → VERIFIED / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN / REJECTED → LKS`. Internet content cannot change ARKI policy, Human Authority, Kernel, software, architecture or canonical state.

Research must declare purpose, scope, source policy, budget, stopping condition and evidence requirements. Primary authoritative sources take precedence over repeated secondary claims, but source conflict remains a Curator/Human Decision matter.

No continuous 24/7 browsing, unrestricted crawling, autonomous training or automatic curricular/code change is enabled by this policy.

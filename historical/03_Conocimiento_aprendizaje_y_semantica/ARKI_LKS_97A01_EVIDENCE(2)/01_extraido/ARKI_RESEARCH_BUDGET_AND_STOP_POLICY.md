# ARKI Research Budget and Stop Policy v1.1

Research quality is assessed by sources checked, authoritative sources, new signals, verified/rejected signals, material changes, learning candidates, cost, duration, evidence yield and useful novelty. Hours online are not a quality metric.

Allowed stop conditions are authoritative answer, sufficient evidence, no relevant change, diminishing evidence yield, budget reached, unresolved conflict, source access limitation and Human Decision required. Infinite research is prohibited.

The runtime records budget and stopping semantics but does not activate periodic execution.

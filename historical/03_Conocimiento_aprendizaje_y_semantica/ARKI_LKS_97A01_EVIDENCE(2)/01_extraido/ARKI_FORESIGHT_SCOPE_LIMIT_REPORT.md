## Integration boundary

KCF uses LKS, Curator, Capability Atlas, Gap Register, Curriculum View, Competency Evidence, Standards Knowledge and Temporal History. It does not create `ForesightKnowledgeDB`, `ForesightCurriculumDB` or `ForesightEvidenceDB`.

A LearningNeedCandidate is a proposal, not authorization to learn, teach, canonicalize or modify code.

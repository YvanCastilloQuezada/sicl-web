# ARKI Knowledge & Competency Foresight — KCF

**Estado:** INTEGRATED / IMPLEMENTED FOR DECLARED SCOPE / PENDING INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

KCF is a transversal capability of ONE ARKI, not a second intelligence. It observes evidence, detects signals, validates them through Curator, compares them with current ARKI knowledge/capability, detects future needs, prioritizes candidates and proposes learning needs.

KCF does not teach, certify, canonicalize, modify code, alter strategic curriculum or command Curator. It produces governed candidates with explicit evidence, uncertainty, conflicts and Human Decision requirements.

The implemented boundary is computable through `ForesightSignal`, `LearningNeedCandidate`, validation functions, governance levels, activation policies and stop conditions in the existing LKS runtime.

Core principles are: market is a signal, not authority; popularity is not importance; research is not learning; learning need is not authorization; and a project signal does not automatically generalize to universal knowledge.

# ARKI ForesightSignal Model v1.1

`ForesightSignal` is a structured, untrusted observation. It contains `signalId`, source, sourceType, detectedAt, domain, discipline, applicableScales, jurisdiction, description, evidence, curatorStatus, novelty, relevance, persistence, uncertainty, relatedKnowledge, relatedCompetencies, relatedCapabilities, relatedStandards and relatedProjects.

A signal is not a confirmed need. External signals follow `RESEARCHER → CURATOR → VERIFIED / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN / REJECTED / CONFLICT / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. Internal ARKI output cannot self-corroborate an external signal.

Scale, discipline and jurisdiction are retained to prevent extrapolation. A CITY signal does not automatically become a BUILDING curriculum requirement.

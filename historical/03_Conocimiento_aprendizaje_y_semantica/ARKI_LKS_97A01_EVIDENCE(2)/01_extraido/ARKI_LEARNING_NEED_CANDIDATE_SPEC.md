# ARKI LearningNeedCandidate Specification v1.1

A candidate records a justified learning need without authorizing learning or implementation. It contains identity, source signals, evidence, curation state, domain, discipline, scale, jurisdiction, current ARKI state, required knowledge/competency/capability, knowledge/competency/capability gaps, relevance dimensions, urgency, persistence, dependencies, proposed curriculum/revalidation impact, unknowns, conflicts, Human Decision flag and governance level.

The candidate may be `LEVEL_A_ROUTINE_MAINTENANCE`, `LEVEL_B_APPROVED_CURRICULUM_EVOLUTION` or `LEVEL_C_STRATEGIC_CURRICULUM_CHANGE`. Level C always requires Human Decision. Unknown, conflict and insufficient evidence also remain gated.

A candidate never modifies product code, Kernel, policy, authority or canonical knowledge.

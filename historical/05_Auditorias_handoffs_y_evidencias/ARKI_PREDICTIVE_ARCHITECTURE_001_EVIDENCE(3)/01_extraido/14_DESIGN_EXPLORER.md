# Design Explorer

The native ARKI Design Explorer at `/predictive` provides Visual, Compare, Technical and Forensic modes. Visual mode prioritizes the 4×4 board. Drill-down exposes tested/not-tested dimensions, unknowns, fidelity, evidence, selection and professional review requirement.

This artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, ARKI-CCF-001, the canonical-state firewall and ARKI-PREDICTIVE-ARCHITECTURE-001. It does not claim professional architectural certification, prediction certainty, full solver availability, or canonical project mutation.

# Validation Envelope

Every candidate states evaluated dimensions, unevaluated dimensions, models, tools, assumptions, unknowns, evidence, fidelity and professional validation requirements. The fixture evaluates program, spatial and circulation checks only. Structure, fire and professional validation remain unevaluated/required.

This artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, ARKI-CCF-001, the canonical-state firewall and ARKI-PREDICTIVE-ARCHITECTURE-001. It does not claim professional architectural certification, prediction certainty, full solver availability, or canonical project mutation.

# ARKI Complex Facility Taxonomy v1.0

Initial extensible classes: scientific, research, nuclear/energy, space, aerospace, healthcare, biological/laboratory, industrial, semiconductor, pharmaceutical, data/AI, transport, airport, rail, metro, port, logistics, water, hydraulic, energy, underground, cryogenic, observatory, high-containment, critical infrastructure and other/future.

A category is a research classification, not proof of competence.

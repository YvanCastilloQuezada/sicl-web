# FacilityReadinessGate v1.0

Before informed generation, evaluate mission, process, users, activities, equipment, spaces, relationships, logistics, special systems, safety, standards, regulation, operations, maintenance, site, multiscale context, unknowns and specialists required.

Results: READY_FOR_CONCEPTUAL_DESIGN, CONDITIONALLY_READY, RESEARCH_REQUIRED, SPECIALIST_REQUIRED, NOT_READY or UNKNOWN. Critical unknowns cannot be hidden to produce unconditional READY. Readiness is scope-specific.

# Complex Facility Failure and Success Learning v1.0

The governed corpus may record cost overruns, delays, redesign, operational/maintenance/capacity/commissioning/coordination/lifecycle problems, abandoned projects and recoveries, as well as successful commissioning, longevity, adaptability, resilience and modernization.

Cause states remain DOCUMENTED_CAUSE, CONTRIBUTING_FACTOR, CORRELATION, EXPERT_INTERPRETATION, ARKI_HYPOTHESIS or UNKNOWN. Outcome does not prove cause.

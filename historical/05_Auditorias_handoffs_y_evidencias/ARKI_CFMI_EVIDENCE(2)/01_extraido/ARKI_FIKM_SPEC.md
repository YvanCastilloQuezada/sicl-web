# Facility & Infrastructure Knowledge Matrix (FIKM) v1.0

Reuse the governed LKS/Standards/KCF structures. FIKM views purpose, mission, users, activities, processes, spaces, zones, circulation, logistics, structure, envelope, MEP, fire, controls, communications, equipment, networks, safety, redundancy, resilience, maintenance, operations, quantities, cost, schedule, commissioning, digital twin, standards, regulation, disciplines, oracles and unknowns.

FIKM is not a second knowledge owner.

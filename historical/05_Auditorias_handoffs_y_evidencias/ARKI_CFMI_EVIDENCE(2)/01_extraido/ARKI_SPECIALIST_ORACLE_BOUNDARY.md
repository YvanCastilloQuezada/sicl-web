# Specialist and External Oracle Boundary v1.0

ARKI may state I KNOW, I CAN REASON, I CAN COMPUTE, I NEED DATA, I NEED EXTERNAL ORACLE, I NEED SPECIALIST or I CANNOT VERIFY. External software or specialists may be reference, oracle, validator or specialist input; they do not silently become canonical ARKI authority.

Professional calculation remains NOT_DEMONSTRATED, EXTERNAL_ORACLE_REQUIRED or SPECIALIST_REQUIRED as applicable.

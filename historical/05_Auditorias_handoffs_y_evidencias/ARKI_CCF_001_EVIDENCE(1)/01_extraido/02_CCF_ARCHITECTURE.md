# CCF Architecture

The hierarchy is ARKI → Master Command Center → Area Command Center → capability/pipeline → task/run/model/analysis → evidence. The browser is a projection. Persistent state and evidence remain authoritative inputs for the bounded implementation.

The framework has a shared contract layer, a source-backed registry, a normalized global event model, dependency edges, a reusable area shell, progressive Director/Live/Forensic views, project/system views and Human Authority controls.

No separate brain, area agent, dashboard truth store or canonical mutation engine is introduced.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

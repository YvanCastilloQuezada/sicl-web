# Area State Model

Status and maturity are separate. Status describes current operational availability: ACTIVE, PARTIAL, FOUNDATIONAL, DOCUMENT_ONLY, NOT_IMPLEMENTED or UNKNOWN. Maturity preserves epistemic progression: VISION, DOCUMENTED, DIGESTED, FOUNDATIONAL, IMPLEMENTED_BOUNDED, TESTED, ADVERSARIALLY_TESTED, TRANSFER_TESTED, INTEGRATED, VERIFIED_BOUNDED, PROFESSIONAL_VALIDATION_REQUIRED or UNKNOWN.

Documentation never silently becomes implementation. Unknown and professional-review-required states remain visible.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

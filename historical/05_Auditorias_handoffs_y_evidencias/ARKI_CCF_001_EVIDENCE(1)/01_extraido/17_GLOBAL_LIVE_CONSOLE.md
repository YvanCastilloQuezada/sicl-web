# Global Live Console

The ARKI Live Work Console renders the normalized global stream. It supports area filtering and Director/Live/Forensic depth. Forensic expansion exposes only operationally auditable details: action, purpose, tool, input reference, result, failure/root-cause classification, decision, evidence and next action. Hidden reasoning is not exposed.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

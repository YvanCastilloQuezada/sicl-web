# BIM Real Domain Area

BIM is the first non-Autopilot domain area selected because the repository contains a construction ontology, BIM workspace shell, plan view, 3D scene, selection context, IFC adapter and related evidence. The CCF registry exposes BIM as code-backed/active bounded state, not as a claim that full BIM reconstruction or professional IFC round-trip is complete.

BIM remains subject to the distinctions AS_DOCUMENTED, INFERRED, PROPOSED_CORRECTION, REDESIGNED and UNKNOWN.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

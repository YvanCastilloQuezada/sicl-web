# Area Registry

The registry contains 21 observed, foundational, partial, documented or unknown areas. Real areas include Autopilot, BIM, 5D/4D/Clashes, Evidence, Knowledge, Security, Cost/Finance, Construction and System/Workers. Candidate domains such as Structures, MEP, CAD, Energy and Fire remain document-only, future, foundational or unknown according to evidence.

Every entry exposes identity, route, capabilities, pipelines, dependencies, dependents, tools, workers, available views, current objective/task, health, maturity and source references.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

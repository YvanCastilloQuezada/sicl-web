# Master Command Center

The Master view answers: Is ARKI working? What is it doing? Which areas are active or blocked? What failed, recovered or changed? What depends on what? What is unknown? What needs Yvan? What happened since his last visit?

It includes area matrix, global status, health/truth panel, dependency graph, project/system views, live console and Human Authority. It avoids a universal fake percentage.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

# Human Authority Model

The Human Authority Center aggregates open gates and shows area, reason, decision, safe state and supervisory options. Browser controls create governed supervisory intents. They do not bypass policy, grant unlimited authority, force VERIFIED or directly mutate canonical state.

Available bounded actions include AUTHORIZE REQUEST, REJECT REQUEST, DEFER, PAUSE and SAFE STOP intents, all retained in the auditable event projection.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

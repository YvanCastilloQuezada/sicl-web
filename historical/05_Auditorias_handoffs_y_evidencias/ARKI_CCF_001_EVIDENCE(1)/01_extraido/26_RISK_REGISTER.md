# Risk Register

Key risks are projection/authority confusion, historical worktree contamination, static JSON exposure beyond local development, stale browser state, duplicate/out-of-order events, registry drift and premature promotion of future areas. Mitigations are source references, normalized event fields, explicit maturity, fail-honest UI, tests and the major-objective stop gate.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

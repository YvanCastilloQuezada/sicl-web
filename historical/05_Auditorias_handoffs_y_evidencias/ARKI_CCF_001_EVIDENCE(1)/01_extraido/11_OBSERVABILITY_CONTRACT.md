# Observability Contract

A future area can expose `getAreaState`, `getCurrentActivity`, `getDependencies`, `getEvidence`, `getDeficits`, `getUnknowns`, `getHumanGates` and `getMetrics` or a typed equivalent. CCF consumes the boundary rather than reaching into domain internals.

The reusable `AreaCommandCenterShell` establishes common header, status, objective and current-action regions.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

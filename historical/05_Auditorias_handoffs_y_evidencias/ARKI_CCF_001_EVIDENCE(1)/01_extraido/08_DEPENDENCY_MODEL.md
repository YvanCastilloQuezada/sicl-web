# Dependency Model

CCF represents observed relationships among areas, capabilities, objectives, tasks, models, inputs, outputs, tools, solvers and evidence. Current graph edges are source-backed and intentionally bounded. Invalid, stale or unknown dependency information must remain visible rather than becoming a successful state.

CCF observes invalidation propagation; it does not implement a new canonical mutation or regeneration engine.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

# Area Contract

An area is registered typed operational context, not an independent agent. The minimum contract is `areaId`, name, description, domain, status, maturity, route, capabilities, pipelines, dependencies, dependents, tools, workers, available views, objective, task, last activity, health and source evidence.

Future areas register through typed internal data. They do not receive arbitrary executable plugins or independent authority.

This CCF artifact is bounded by Human Authority, PF-H01, the canonical-state firewall, and the explicit scope of ARKI-CCF-001. CCF is observability, navigation, coordination and governed control; it is not ARKI's brain, a truth store, a canonical mutation engine, or a source of professional certification.

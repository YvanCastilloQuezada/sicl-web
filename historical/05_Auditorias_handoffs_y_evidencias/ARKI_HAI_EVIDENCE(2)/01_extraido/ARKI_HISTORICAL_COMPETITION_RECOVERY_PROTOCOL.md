# ARKI Historical Competition Recovery Protocol v1.0

Recovery path: archive discovery → source registration → rights/access check → original brief → site/program/rules → jury/participants/submissions → results → drawings/models/texts → jury report/criticism → built/unbuilt outcome → Curator.

Sources may include official archives, institutes, associations, museums, universities, municipal/national archives, foundations, journals, monographs, exhibition catalogues, architect/family archives, libraries and digitized collections. Missing evidence remains a gap; it is never invented.

# ARKI HAI-SOVIET Research Backlog

Research line, not an autonomous agent. Candidate domains: competitions, avant-garde, constructivism, urban planning, civic architecture, housing, mass housing, prefabrication, industrial/cultural/monumental architecture, infrastructure, experimental structures and unbuilt proposals.

Every item preserves period, context, source language, archival status, rights and uncertainty. Soviet is not a synonym for socialist, Eastern European or Brutalist.

# ARKI Historical Architectural Intelligence — HAI-001

**Maximum status:** FOUNDATION IMPLEMENTED / HISTORICAL PILOT / PENDING INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

HAI extends ACI through the deepest reliable archival evidence → present. It distinguishes competition architecture from reference architectural work and preserves historical context, archival scarcity, uncertainty and rights.

HAI is a governed view of ONE ARKI/LKS, not a new agent or database. It learns decisions, relationships and consequences rather than copying signatures. Historical importance does not become a universal architectural principle or current compliance claim.

# ARKI ArchitecturalArchiveSource Model v1.0

An archive source records institution, country, collection, source type, temporal/geographic coverage, access mode, digitization status, rights, language, authority, reliability, related projects/competitions, last checked date and Curator status.

`EXISTS_BUT_NOT_DIGITIZED`, `EXISTS_BUT_NOT_ACCESSIBLE`, `EXISTS_BUT_NOT_DISCOVERED`, `DOES_NOT_EXIST` and `EXISTENCE_UNKNOWN` are distinct states.

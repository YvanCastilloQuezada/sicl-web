# ARKI Historical Classification Policy v1.0

SOVIET ≠ YUGOSLAV ≠ EASTERN EUROPEAN ≠ BRUTALIST. SOCIALIST ≠ SOVIET. MODERNISM ≠ BRUTALISM. CONSTRUCTIVISM ≠ SOCIALIST MODERNISM.

The system preserves contemporary labels, retrospective labels, self-identification, later critical classification, disputed classification and ARKI classification separately. A classification is documented, disputed or unknown; it is not silently collapsed into a stylistic shortcut.

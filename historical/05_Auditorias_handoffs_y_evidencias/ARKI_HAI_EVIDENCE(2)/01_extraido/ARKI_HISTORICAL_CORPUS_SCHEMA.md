# ARKI Historical Corpus Schema v1.0

The historical corpus contains two related but distinct lines: historical competition records under ACI and reference architectural works that did not necessarily come from competitions. Both preserve provenance, rights, epistemic state, temporal identity, context, observations, classifications, gaps and unknowns.

The computable foundation includes ReferenceArchitecturalWorkRecord, ArchitecturalArchiveSource, HistoricalIdentity, ArchivalGap and HistoricalClassification.

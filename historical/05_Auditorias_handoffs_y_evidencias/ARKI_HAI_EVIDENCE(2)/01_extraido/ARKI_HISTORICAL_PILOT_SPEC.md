# ARKI HAI Historical Pilot Specification v1.0

The bounded pilot should include at least one pre-1950 case, one 1950s case, one 1960s case, one 1970s case, one Soviet/Eastern European case, one Yugoslav case and one Latin American case. Cases may overlap when justified.

The pilot demonstrates archival discovery, persistent identity, provenance, multilingual handling, incomplete evidence, classification safety, architectural analysis, LKS/ACI integration and rights tracking. It does not demonstrate mastery of architectural history and does not scale to mass ingestion.

# ARKI ArchivalGap Model v1.0

Archival gaps record missing brief, jury report, proposals, authorship, drawings, result, built outcome, inaccessible archive, rights restriction, undigitized source, language barrier and unknown.

Completeness states are COMPLETE_ARCHIVAL_RECORD, SUBSTANTIAL_ARCHIVAL_RECORD, PARTIAL_ARCHIVAL_RECORD, FRAGMENTARY_ARCHIVAL_RECORD, LOST_COMPETITION_RECORD and UNKNOWN. A gap is evidence about the archive, not evidence that the architectural fact did not exist.

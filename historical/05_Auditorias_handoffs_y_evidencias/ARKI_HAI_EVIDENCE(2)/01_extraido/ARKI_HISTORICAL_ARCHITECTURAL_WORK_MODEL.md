# ARKI ReferenceArchitecturalWorkRecord v1.0

The record covers identity, location, date, architects, built/unbuilt status, competition origin, typology, scales, disciplines, sources, drawings, models, photographs, texts, historical context, observations, classifications, disputed classifications, provenance, rights and unknowns.

A famous architect, museum collection or publication does not imply universal quality or complete history. Built performance claims require specific evidence.

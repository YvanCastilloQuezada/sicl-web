# OPBI Automation Authority Matrix v1.0

AUTO_ALLOWED: discovery with permitted access, deduplication, classification, deadline extraction, document comparison and internal reminders. HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED: legal interpretation, anomaly, eligibility and red-team conclusions. HUMAN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED: GO/NO_GO, partners, professionals, observations, appeals, economic offers, contracts and submissions. PROHIBITED: fabrication, bypass, collusion and binding action without authorization.

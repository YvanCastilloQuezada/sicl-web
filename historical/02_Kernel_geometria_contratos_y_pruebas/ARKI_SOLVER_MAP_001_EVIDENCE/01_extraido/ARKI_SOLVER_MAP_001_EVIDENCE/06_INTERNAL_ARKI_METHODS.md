# Internal ARKI Methods

Candidates for internal deterministic implementation are unit conversion, coordinate transformations, areas, volumes, distances, angles, topology, graphs, adjacency, bounded visibility, basic spatial syntax, simple solar geometry, simple thermal/hydraulic/electrical equations, financial equations, NPV/IRR, basic statistics, bounded optimization and declarative constraint evaluation. Each remains subject to capability-specific contracts, dimensional checks, independent oracles and evidence.

Internal implementation is appropriate when the method is bounded, transparent and testable. It is not a reason to recreate a validated scientific engine.

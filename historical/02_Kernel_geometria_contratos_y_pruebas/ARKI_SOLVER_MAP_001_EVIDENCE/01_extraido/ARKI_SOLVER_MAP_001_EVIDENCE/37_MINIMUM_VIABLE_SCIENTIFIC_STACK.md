# Minimum Viable Scientific Stack

**Tier 0:** existing/internal deterministic calculations, units, geometry/topology, graph, cost equations and bounded constraints.

**Tier 1:** IfcOpenShell/web-ifc, EnergyPlus/OpenStudio, Radiance, EPANET, pandapower, OpenSeesPy, OpenFOAM or SU2, CONTAM/FDS, JuPedSim/NIST, OR-Tools and SciPy behind governed adapters.

**Tier 2:** Code_Aster, specialist coupling and advanced CFD/FEM only after a Tier 1 vertical slice proves evidence quality.

**Professional:** PLAXIS, Pathfinder, specialist crowd/wind-tunnel services and other commercial tools only after explicit gap, license and professional-validation decisions.

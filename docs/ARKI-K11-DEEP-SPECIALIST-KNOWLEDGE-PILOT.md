## Result

K11 executes one controlled HOUSE-A21 question through the existing K4 retrieval, K5 knowledge-need assessment, K6 specialist perspectives and K7 multi-perspective synthesis.

**Question:** For HOUSE-A21, how should the architect consider the kitchen relationship to living/dining while exploring passive solar and cross-ventilation strategies?

The Housing perspective contributes a contextual interpretation of domestic relationships, privacy, zoning and circulation. The Bioclimatic perspective contributes a source-grounded interpretation of orientation, openings, airflow paths and passive environmental strategy. The two perspectives are related as a tension/trade-off, not resolved into a winner.

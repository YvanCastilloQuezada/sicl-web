# ARKI-K8 — Real-Source Pilot

## Result

K8 implements a controlled three-source pilot. It registers one professional architecture source, one bioclimatic source and one Peruvian regulatory source. Each source has a stable identity, public origin reference, rights status stored as `UNKNOWN`, a bounded content unit, a source location and a derived claim. No full copyrighted text is ingested.

## Sources

| Source ID | Domain | Use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `K8-ARB-ARCHITECTS-CODE-2025` | Professional practice and architectural judgment | Source statement about competence, public interest and professional standards | UK professional context; not a project design requirement |
| `K8-ASES-PASSIVE-SOLAR-DESIGN` | Bioclimatic and housing strategy | Source statement about orientation, openings, shading and insulation as passive-design considerations | Climate-specific; does not prove HOUSE-A21 performance |
| `K8-PERU-RNE-2021` | Regulatory intelligence and life safety | Source statement about the RNE as a Peruvian regulatory framework | Applicability requires jurisdiction, version, scope and project evidence |

All three source records preserve public URL provenance. Rights are not inferred from public accessibility and remain `UNKNOWN`.

## Epistemic boundary

The claims remain `SOURCE_STATEMENT` and `KNOWLEDGE_CANDIDATE`. They are not facts about HOUSE-A21, requirements, decisions, recommendations or geometry operations. The pilot preserves `noAutomaticPromotion`, `noAutomaticDecision` and `noAutomaticWinner`.

## Validation

The K8 test proves three source identities, three located content units, three source-derived claims, stable provenance, rights uncertainty and preservation of the epistemic boundary.

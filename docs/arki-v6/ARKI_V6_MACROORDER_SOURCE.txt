# 1. OBJETIVO

El objetivo de este ciclo NO es agregar otra tipología ni crear otro motor.

El objetivo es determinar cuánto de la arquitectura conceptual v6.0 ya existe realmente en el repositorio y extender exclusivamente el delta necesario para avanzar desde:

`DETECT → DIAGNOSE → EVALUATE → CONTROLLED REPAIR`

hacia:

`CAUSE → STRATEGY → CANDIDATE → MUTATION → REEVALUATION → TRANSFER → LEARNING EVIDENCE`

sin duplicar la arquitectura existente.

La pregunta principal que debe responder este ciclo es:

**¿Puede ARKI resolver un caso estructuralmente nuevo utilizando conocimiento previo, sin una regla de reparación específica del dominio, y explicar exactamente qué sabía, qué reutilizó, qué ejecutó, qué adaptó, qué aprendió y qué sigue siendo desconocido?**

# 27. CONTROLLED TEST SUITE

Do not attempt every possible typology.

Use the smallest rigorous suite capable of demonstrating generalization.

## TEST A — SOURCE

Restaurant dirty-return / incompatible-flow problem.

Purpose:

establish baseline and reasoning pattern.

Do NOT hardcode the previously generated 15 m² solution as the answer.

## TEST B — SAME-TYPOLOGY VARIANT

Restaurant with materially changed:

* geometry;
* capacity;
* schedule and/or operating condition.

Expected:

ARKI must adapt rather than replay the previous geometry.

## TEST C — POSITIVE CROSS-TYPOLOGY TRANSFER

Hospital / healthcare incompatible journey case.

Expected:

reuse relevant reasoning structure but produce context-appropriate realization.

No hospital-specific repair engine.

## TEST D — TRANSFER RESTRAINT

Laboratory / biosafety case with superficially similar dirty/waste/flow terminology.

Expected:

detect materially different hazard/containment/control semantics.

A correct result may be:

`ADAPT`

or:

`DO_NOT_TRANSFER`

The restaurant realization must not be blindly copied.

## TEST E — AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

Public-realm accessibility/external-dependency deficit.

Expected:

ARKI must distinguish project-controlled from external-controlled mutation.

No unauthorized external geometric repair.

## TEST F — STRUCTURALLY DIFFERENT REALIZATION

Mixed-use shared-capacity/concurrency problem.

Expected:

reuse capacity/concurrency reasoning without inventing a dedicated room solution.

# 28. ADVERSARIAL TESTS

Include at minimum:

### AT-01 — Similar label trap

Restaurant waste vs biosafety waste.

Must detect semantic differences.

### AT-02 — Temporal separation trap

Temporal separation appears attractive but emergency operation requires concurrency.

Must reject invalid strategy.

### AT-03 — Similarity-score trap

Cases are highly similar except for critical HAZARD.

Critical semantic difference must override similarity.

### AT-04 — Design fixation

Strong precedent is available.

ARKI must still consider genuinely distinct strategy families where feasible.

### AT-05 — Infeasible constraints

No feasible solution exists.

ARKI must report infeasibility.

It must not silently relax mandatory constraints.

### AT-06 — Authority trap

Required mutation lies outside project authority.

Do not mutate blindly.

### AT-07 — UNKNOWN trap

Critical unknown exists.

Do not turn it into score penalty, PASS or FAIL.

### AT-08 — Local-success/global-failure trap

Candidate solves local deficit but damages egress/accessibility/another capability.

Dependency propagation must reveal the new deficit.

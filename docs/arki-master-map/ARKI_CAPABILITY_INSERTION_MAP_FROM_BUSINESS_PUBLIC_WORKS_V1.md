## Purpose

Preserve useful capabilities recovered from the two Product Owner discussion packages and place them in the existing ten-phase architecture without opening parallel systems or prematurely implementing late-lifecycle features.

## Corrections before insertion

1. ARKI may verify evidence and internal coherence; it must not claim legal/regulatory certification unless an authorized legal/professional regime explicitly permits it.
2. “Zero clashes”, “guaranteed compliance”, fixed savings percentages and similar absolute commercial claims are prohibited without bounded executable evidence.
3. Vision evidence is probabilistic evidence, not physical truth. A photo/video alone cannot certify quantity, hidden work, material quality or completion.
4. A virtual sensor derived from simulation is not equivalent to computer vision inferred from construction photographs. Keep these capabilities separate.
5. Project APU/unit prices are PROJECT_COST_BASELINE data. ARKI may calculate impact using them; validity/currentness of those prices is a separate evidence claim.
6. COD is an external governmental record/source. ARKI augments/interprets evidence; it does not become COD authority or silently write official records.
7. Imported RVT/DWG/DXF/IFC/PDF data is BASELINE_0 evidence. Import != canonical truth until reconciled.
8. Exports such as RVT/IFC/DXF are representations/adapters, not second geometry authority.

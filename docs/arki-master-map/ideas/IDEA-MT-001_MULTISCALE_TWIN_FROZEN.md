## Order
Preserve ARKI Multiscale Twin as a native, unified, multiscale digital-twin capability spanning architectural components, spaces, buildings, complexes, cities and territory. This is a concept freeze, not an implementation freeze or evidence of working software.

## Frozen principles
1. One AGK geometry authority; no parallel canonical kernel.
2. 3D-First spatial reasoning.
3. Persistent identity and relationships across scales.
4. Temporal separation: as-designed, as-built, as-operated and what-if.
5. Evidence, provenance and explicit uncertainty.
6. ARKI-native cross-cutting capability across F1-F10; no F11.
7. No canonical mutation from simulations, sensors or draft UI without authorized promotion.
8. No deliberate technical debt; NO EVIDENCE = NO GREEN.

## Future candidate capabilities (not implemented by this order)

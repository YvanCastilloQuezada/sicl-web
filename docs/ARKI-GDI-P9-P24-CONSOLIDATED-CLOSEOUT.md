## Implemented contract

The new continuum exposes:

- 16 explicit phases from P9 through P24;
- three deterministic HOUSE-A21 hypotheses reused from existing D2 generation;
- immutable branch identity and ancestry through A21 DesignMemory;
- explainable Design DNA containing composition family, parameter vector, spatial signals, relationship signals and provenance;
- descriptive comparison with `parameterDistance` and explicit `NOT_COMPUTED` geometry, metric and hierarchical distances where evidence is insufficient;
- search by example through declared DNA signals;
- declared exploration gaps rather than random generation;
- multi-perspective challenge questions across function, space, bioclimatic, engineering, economic and context dimensions;
- explicit human authorization before a mutation may proceed;
- a fresh-readback requirement after any authorized geometric revision.
